由于 \(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_{2n}\) (\(n \geq 2\)) 相互独立同分布，则 \(X_i\) 与 \(X_2\)，\(X_i\) 与 \(X_{n+i}\)，\(\overline{X}_i\) 与 \(\overline{X}_2\) 也独立 (\(i=1,2,\cdots,n\))。而由独立随机变量期望的性质（若随机变量 \(X, Y\) 独立，且 \(EX, EY\) 都存在，则 \(EXY = EXEY\)），所以

\[EX_i X_{n+i} = EX_i EX_{n+i} = u^2, \quad EX_i \overline{X}_2 = EX_i E\overline{X}_2 = u^2\]

\[E\overline{X}_i X_{n+i} = E\overline{X}_i EX_{n+i} = u^2, \quad E\overline{X}_i \overline{X}_2 = E\overline{X}_i E\overline{X}_2 = u^2\]

故有

\[E\left\{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\left[\left(X_i - \overline{X}_1\right)\left(X_{n+i} - \overline{X}_2\right)\right]\right\}\]

\[= \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left( EX_i X_{n+i} - EX_i \overline{X}_2 - E\overline{X}_1 X_{n+i} + E\overline{X}_1 \overline{X}_2 \right) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left( u^2 - u^2 - u^2 + u^2 \right) = 0\]
