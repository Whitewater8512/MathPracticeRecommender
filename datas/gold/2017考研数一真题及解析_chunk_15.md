令 \( E(Z_{i}) = \bar{Z} \)

\[ \bar{Z} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} Z_{i} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} |X_{i} - \mu| \]

由此可得 \( \sigma \) 的矩估计量 \( \hat{\sigma} = \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{2}} \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} |X_{i} - \mu| \)

对总体 \( X \) 的 \( n \) 个样本 \( X_{1}, X_{2}, \ldots, X_{n} \), 则相应的绝对误差的样本 \( Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \ldots, Z_{n}, Z_{i} = |X_{i} - \mu|, i = 1, 2, \ldots, n \), 令其样本值为 \( Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \ldots, Z_{n}, Z_{i} = |X_{i} - \mu| \)

则对应的似然函数 \( L(\sigma) = \begin{cases} 
\left( \frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} \right)^{n} e^{-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2} \sum_{i=1}^{n} Z_{i}^2}, & Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \ldots, Z_{n} > 0 \\
0, & 其他 
\end{cases} \)

两边取对数，当 \( Z_{1}, Z_{2}, \ldots, Z_{n} > 0 \) 时

\[ \ln L(\sigma) = n \ln \frac{2}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2} \sum_{i=1}^{n} Z_{i}^2 \]
