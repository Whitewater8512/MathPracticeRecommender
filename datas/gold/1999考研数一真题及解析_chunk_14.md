$\lambda_0 \begin{bmatrix} -a+1+c \\ -5-b+3 \\ -(1-c)-a \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \lambda_0(-a+1+c) \\ \lambda_0(-5-b+3) \\ \lambda_0[-(1-c)-a] \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

矩阵相等，则矩阵的对应元素都相同，可得

$\begin{cases} \lambda_0(-a+1+c) = 1 & (1) \\ \lambda_0(-5-b+3) = 1 & (2) \\ \lambda_0[-(1-c)-a] = -1 & (3) \end{cases}$
