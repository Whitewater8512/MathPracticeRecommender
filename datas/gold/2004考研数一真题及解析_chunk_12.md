①式对$y$求导：
$$
-6-\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}-2y\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}-2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\cdot\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}-2z\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}=0,
$$

②式对$y$求导：
$$
20-2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}-2\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}-2y\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}-2(\frac{\partial z}{\partial y})^{2}-2z\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}=0,
$$

将
$$
\begin{cases}
x=9, \\
y=3, \\
z=3
\end{cases}
$$
代入，于是$A=\left.\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}}\right|_{(9,3,3)}=\frac{1}{6}$，$B=\left.\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x\partial y}\right|_{(9,3,3)}=-\frac{1}{2}$，

$C=\left.\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}\right|_{(9,3,3)}=\frac{5}{3}$，故$AC-B^{2}=\frac{1}{36}>0$，又$A=\frac{1}{6}>0$，从而点(9,3)是$z(x,y)$的极小值点，极小值为$z(9,3)=3$.
