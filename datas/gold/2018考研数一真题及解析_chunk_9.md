(18)(I)解:通解 $y(x)=e^{-\int_{1}^{4x}\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{\int_{1}^{4x}dx+C}}$

$=e^{-x}(\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{\int_{1}^{4x}dx+C})$

$=e^{-x}[(x-1)e^{x}+C]$

$=(x-1)+Ce^{-x}$.

(II)证明:设 $f(x+T)=f(x)$,即 $T$ 是 $f(x)$ 的周期.

通解 $y(x)=e^{-\int_{1}^{4x}\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{\int_{1}^{4x}dx+C}}$

$=e^{-x}(\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{\int_{1}^{4x}dx+C})$

$=e^{-x}\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{x}dx+Ce^{-x}$.

不妨设 $y(x)=e^{-x}\int_{1}^{4x}f(x)e^{x}dx+Ce^{-x}$,则有

$y(x+T)=e^{-(x+T)}\int_{1}^{4x+T}f(t)e^{t}dt+Ce^{-(x+T)}$

$=e^{-(x+T)}\int_{0}^{4x}f(u+T)e^{u+T}du+(Ce^{-T})\cdot e^{-x}$

$=e^{-(x+T)}\int_{0}^{4x}f(u)e^{u}\cdot e^{T}du+(Ce^{-T})\cdot e^{-x}$

$=e^{-x}\int_{0}^{4x}f(u)e^{u}du+(Ce^{-T})\cdot e^{-x}$,

即 $y(x+T)$ 依旧是方程的通解,结论得证.
