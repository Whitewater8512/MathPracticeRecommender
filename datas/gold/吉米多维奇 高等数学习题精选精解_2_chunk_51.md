### 【741】设 u = \arcsin \frac{x}{x + y}, 则 du = \frac{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - (\frac{x}{x + y})^2}} \cdot (x + y)dx - x(dx + dy)}{(x + y)^2}.

#### 解
$$du = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - (\frac{x}{x + y})^2}} \cdot \frac{(x + y)dx - x(dx + dy)}{(x + y)^2}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x + y)^2 - x^2}} \cdot \frac{x - x(dx + dy) + dx}{x^2}$$

$$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x + y)^2 - x^2}} \cdot \frac{x + y(dx + dy) + dx}{x^2}.$$

故应填 \frac{1}{\sqrt{(x + y)^2 - x^2}} \cdot \frac{x + y(dx + dy) + dx}{x^2}.
