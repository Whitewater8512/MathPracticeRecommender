方法二：
\[
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(\cos x)}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+\cos x - 1)}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\cos x - 1}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{-\frac{1}{2}x^2}{x^2} = -\frac{1}{2}.
\]
