# 填空题

## (1) 【答案】
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{e}}$$

【详解】方法1：求 $\lim_{x \to 0} u(x)^{v(x)}$ 型极限，一般先化为指数形式

$$\lim_{x \to 0} u(x)^{v(x)} = \lim_{x \to 0} e^{v(x) \ln u(x)}$$

然后求 $\lim_{x \to 0} v(x) \ln u(x)$，再回到指数上去.

$$\lim_{x \to 0} (\cos x)^{\ln(1+x^2)} = \lim_{x \to 0} e^{\ln(\cos x) \ln(1+x^2)} = e^{\lim_{x \to 0} \ln(\cos x) \ln(1+x^2)},$$

而

$$\lim_{x \to 0} \ln(\cos x) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+\cos x - 1)}{\ln(1+x^2)} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\cos x - 1}{x^2} \quad (\text{等价无穷小替换} \ln(1+x) \sim x)$$

$$= \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1 - \frac{x^2}{2}}{x^2} = -\frac{1}{2} \quad (\text{等价无穷小替换} 1 - \cos x \sim \frac{1}{2} x^2)$$

故 原式 $= e^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{e}}.$
