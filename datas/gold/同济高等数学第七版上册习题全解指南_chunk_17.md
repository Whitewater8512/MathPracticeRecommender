\[ b = 32, \quad m = \frac{212 - 32}{100} = 1.8. \]

故 \(F = 1.8C + 32\) 或 \(C = \frac{5}{9}(F - 32)\).

(1) \(F = 90^\circ\), \(C = \frac{5}{9}(90 - 32) \approx 32.2^\circ\).

\(C = -5^\circ\), \(F = 1.8 \times (-5) + 32 = 23^\circ\).

(2) 设温度值 \(t\) 符合题意, 则有

\[ t = 1.8t + 32, \quad t = -40. \]

即华氏 \(-40^\circ\) 恰好也是摄氏 \(-40^\circ\).
