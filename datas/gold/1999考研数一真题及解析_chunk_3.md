$\begin{aligned}
&\frac{d}{dx} \int_0^x \sin(x - t)^2 dt = \frac{d}{dx} \int_0^x (-\sin u^2) du = \frac{d}{dx} \int_0^x \sin u^2 du = \sin x^2
\end{aligned}$
