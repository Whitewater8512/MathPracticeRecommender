\iiint_{\Omega} z \left( x^2 + y^2 + z^2 \right) dV = 4 \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} d\theta \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} d\phi \int_0^{2R \cos \phi} r^5 \cdot r \sin \phi \cos \phi dr
$$

= \frac{64}{3} \pi R^6 \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \cos^7 \phi \sin \phi d\phi = \frac{8}{3} \pi R^6
$$

故 $\bar{z} = \frac{5}{4} R$。因此，球体 $\Omega$ 的重心位置为 $\left( 0, 0, \frac{5}{4} R \right)$
