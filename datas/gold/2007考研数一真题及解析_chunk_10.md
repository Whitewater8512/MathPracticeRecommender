$$e - \frac{1}{2} e^2 - \left(e - e^{\frac{1}{2}}\right) = \frac{1}{2} e^{\frac{1}{2}} = \frac{\sqrt{e}}{2}$$

## (12) 【答案】$f_1'(x^v, y^v) x^{v-1} + f_2'(x^v, y^v) y^v \ln y$

【详解】$\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial f(x^v, y^v)}{\partial x} = f_1'(x^v, y^v) \frac{\partial x^v}{\partial x} + f_2'(x^v, y^v) \frac{\partial y^v}{\partial x} = f_1'(x^v, y^v) x^{v-1} + f_2'(x^v, y^v) y^v \ln y$

## (13) 【答案】$C_1 e^x + C_2 e^{3x} - 2e^{2x}$

【详解】这是二阶常系数非齐次线性微分方程，且函数$f(x)$是$P_n(x) e^{\lambda x}$型其中$P_n(x) = 2, \lambda = 2$.

所给方程对应的齐次方程为$y'' - 4y' + 3y = 0$，它的特征方程为$r^2 - 4r + 3 = 0$，得特征根$r_1 = 1, r_2 = 3$，对应齐次方程的通解$y = C_1 e^x + C_2 e^{3x}$.
