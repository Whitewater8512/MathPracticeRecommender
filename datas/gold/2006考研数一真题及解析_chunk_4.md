$$\Rightarrow \ln y = \ln x - x + c \Rightarrow e^{\ln y} = e^{\ln x - x + c} \Rightarrow y = Cxe^{-x}$$

### (3) 【答案】$2\pi$

【详解】补一个曲面$\Sigma_1: \left\{\begin{array}{l}x^2 + y^2 \leq 1,\\ z = 1\end{array}\right.$取上侧，则$\Sigma_1 + \Sigma$组成的封闭立体$\Omega$满足高斯公式，

$$\iiint_{\Omega} \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z}\right) dv = \oiint_{\Sigma + \Sigma_1} P dy dz + Q dz dx + R dx dy = I$$

设$P = x, Q = 2y, R = 3(z-1)$，则$\frac{\partial P}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial Q}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial R}{\partial z} = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6$

$$\therefore I = \iiint_{\Omega} 6 dx dy dz (\Omega为锥面\Sigma和平面\Sigma_1所围区域) = 6V (V为上述圆锥体积)$$

注：以下几种解法针对不同的方法求圆锥体积$V$

方法1: $I = 6 \times \pi = 2\pi$ (高中方法，圆锥的体积公式，这种方法最简便)
