(1)  
【答案】：C

$\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x^2 + x}{x^2 - 1} = 1$，所以$y = 1$为水平的，没有斜渐近线 故两条选C

【答案】：C  
【解析】：$f'(x) = e^x(e^{2x} - 2) \cdots (e^{nx} - n) + (e^x - 1)(2e^{2x} - 2) \cdots (e^{nx} - n) + \cdots + (e^x - 1)(e^{2x} - 2) \cdots (ne^{nx} - n)$  
所以$f'(0) = (-1)^{n+1} n!$  
(3)  
【答案】：  
【解析】：由于$f(x, y)$在$(0, 0)$处连续，可知如果$\lim_{x \to 0, y \to 0} \frac{f(x, y)}{x^2 + y^2}$存在，则必有$f(0, 0) = \lim_{x \to 0} f(x, y) = 0$  
这样，$\lim_{x \to 0, y \to 0} \frac{f(x, y)}{x^2 + y^2}$就可以写成$\lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x, \Delta y) - f(0, 0)}{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}$，也即极限$\lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x, \Delta y) - f(0, 0)}{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}$存在，可知$\lim_{\Delta y \to 0} \frac{f(\Delta x, \Delta y) - f(0, 0)}{\sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2}} = 0$，也即$f(\Delta x, \Delta y) - f(0, 0) = 0 \Delta x + 0 \Delta y + o(\sqrt{\Delta x^2 + \Delta y^2})$。由可微的定义可知$f(x, y)$在$(0, 0)$处可微。
