整理后得
\[
\begin{cases}
-x f'(x, y) \frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{dz}{dx} = f(x, y) + y f'(x, y) \\
F_y \frac{dy}{dx} + F'_z \frac{dz}{dx} = -F'_x
\end{cases}
\]

解此方程组，得
\[
\frac{dz}{dx} = \frac{\begin{vmatrix} -y f' & f + y f' \\ -x f' & 1 \end{vmatrix}}{\begin{vmatrix} F_y & F'_z \\ F'_y & F'_z \end{vmatrix}} = \frac{(f + y f') F'_y - x F'_z}{F'_y + y F'_z}, (F'_y + y F'_z \neq 0)
\]
