### 基本题型

#### 求函数在某点沿某方向的方向导数

**[790]** 函数 \(u = \ln(x + \sqrt{y^2 + z^2})\) 在 \(A(1, 0, 1)\) 点处沿 \(A\) 点指向 \(B(3, -2, 2)\) 点方向的方向导数为

解 \(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{x + \sqrt{y^2 + z^2}}, \frac{\partial u}{\partial y} = \frac{y}{x + \sqrt{y^2 + z^2}}, \frac{\partial u}{\partial z} = \frac{z}{x + \sqrt{y^2 + z^2}}\)

\[
\left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right|_{A} = \frac{1}{2}, \quad \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right|_{A} = 0, \quad \left.\frac{\partial u}{\partial z}\right|_{A} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

而 \(AB = |2, -2, 1|\), \(\cos\alpha = \frac{2}{3}, \cos\beta = -\frac{2}{3}, \cos\gamma = \frac{1}{3}\).
