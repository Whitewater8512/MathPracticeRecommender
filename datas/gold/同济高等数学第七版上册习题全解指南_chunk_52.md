(3) 因为 \(\alpha \sim \beta\)，\(\beta \sim \gamma\)，即 \(\lim_{x \to a} \frac{\alpha}{\beta} = 1\)，\(\lim_{x \to a} \frac{\beta}{\gamma} = 1\)，所以

\[
\lim_{x \to a} \frac{\alpha}{\gamma} = \lim_{x \to a} \left( \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \cdot \frac{\beta}{\gamma} \right) = \lim_{x \to a} \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \cdot \lim_{x \to a} \frac{\beta}{\gamma} = 1，即 \alpha \sim \gamma.
\]
