而$\oiint_{\Sigma_1} x dy dz + 2y dz dx + 3(z-1) dx dy = 0$ (在$\Sigma_1$上: $z = 1, dz = 0$)

方法2: 先二重积分，后定积分.

因为$V = \int_0^1 S dz, \quad r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}, \quad r^2 = x^2 + y^2, \quad r^2 = z^2, \quad S = \pi r^2 = \pi z^2$,

所以$V = \int_0^1 \pi z^2 dz = \frac{1}{3}\pi z^3 \bigg|_0^1 = \frac{1}{3}\pi$. 从而$I = 6V = 6 \times \frac{\pi}{3} = 2\pi$
