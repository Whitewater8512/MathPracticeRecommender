## (5) \(\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{(x + h)^2 - x^2}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} \frac{h(2x + h)}{h} = \lim_{h \to 0} (2x + h) = 2x.\)

## (6) \(\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(2 - \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2}\right) = \lim_{x \to \infty} 2 - \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x} + \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x^2} = 2 - 0 + 0 = 2.\)

## (7) \(\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x^2 - 1}{2x^2 - x - 1} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1 - \frac{1}{x^2}}{2 - \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{x^2}} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{2} \left(1 - \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{x^2}\right) = \frac{1}{2}.\)

## (8) \(\lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{x^2 + x}{x^4 - 3x^2 + 1} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1 + \frac{1}{x}}{x^2 - 3 + \frac{1}{x^2}} = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{1}{x^2} \left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right) = 0 = 0.\)
