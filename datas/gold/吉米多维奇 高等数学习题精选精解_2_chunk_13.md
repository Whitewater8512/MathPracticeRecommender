解：
由于分母为一项，分子为两项，应先考虑分成两项，再用凑微分及分部积分法积分。
$$
\int \frac{x + \ln^2 x}{(x \ln x)^2} \, dx = \int \frac{1}{x \ln^2 x} \, dx + \int \frac{\ln x}{x^2} \, dx = \int \frac{1}{\ln x} \, d(\ln x) + \int \ln x \, d\left(-\frac{1}{x}\right)
$$
$$
= -\frac{1}{\ln x} - \frac{1}{x} \ln x + \int \frac{1}{x^2} \, dx = -\frac{1}{\ln x} - \frac{1}{x} \ln x - \frac{1}{x} + C.
$$
