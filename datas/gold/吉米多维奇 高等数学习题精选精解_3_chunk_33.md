【948】$\oint_{L}\frac{(x+y)dx-(x-y)dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$,其中$L$为圆周$x^{2}+y^{2}=a^{2}($按逆时针方向绕行$).$

解 $L$的参数方程为$\begin{cases}x=a\cos t,\\y=a\sin t,\end{cases}t:0\rightarrow2\pi.$

$$\oint_{L}\frac{(x+y)dx-(x-y)dy}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$$

$$=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\left[(a\cos t+a\sin t)(a\cos t)^{\prime}-(a\cos t-a\sin t)(a\sin t)^{\prime}\right]dt$$

$$=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\int_{0}^{2\pi}(-a^{2})dt=-2\pi.$$

【949】$\int_{\Gamma}x^{2}dx+xdy-ydz$,其中$\Gamma$为曲线$x=kt,y=a\cos\theta,z=a\sin\theta$上从$\theta=0$到$\theta=\pi$的一段弧.

解 $\int_{\Gamma}x^{2}dx+xdy-ydz=\int_{0}^{\pi}[(kt)^{2}(kt)^{\prime}+a\sin\theta(a\cos\theta)^{\prime}-a\cos\theta(a\sin\theta)^{\prime}]d\theta$

$$=\int_{0}^{\pi}(k^{3}t^{2}-a^{2})d\theta=\frac{1}{3}\pi k^{3}-\pi a^{2}.$$
