**解：** 对方程两边同时微分得
\[ d(xz) + d\left(\sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}\right) = 0. \]
\[ yz dx + xz dy + xy dz + x dx + y dy + z dz = 0. \]
将 \( x = 1, y = 0, z = -1 \) 代入上式，化简并整理后得 \( dz = dx - \sqrt{2} dy. \)
