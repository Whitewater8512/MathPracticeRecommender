证：
(1) 令 x = tan t，即 t = arctan x，当 x→0 时，t→0。
因为
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\arctan x}{x} = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{t}{\tan t} = 1,
$$
所以
$$
\arctan x \sim x \quad (x \to 0).
$$
(2) 因为
$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sec x - 1}{x^2} = \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{1 - \cos x}{x^2} \cdot \frac{1}{\cos x} \right) = \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{2 \sin^2 \frac{x}{2}}{x^2} \cdot \frac{1}{\cos x} \right)
$$
$$
= \lim_{x \to 0} \left( \frac{\sin^2 \frac{x}{2}}{x^2} \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{\cos x} \right) = 1,
$$
所以
$$
\sec x - 1 \sim \frac{x^2}{2} \quad (x \to 0).
$$
