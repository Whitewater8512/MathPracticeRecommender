由单位向量 $n$ 知，$\cos\alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}, \cos\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}, \cos\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}.$

$= \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} + \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} + \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$

故应填 $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{3}.$
