\]

\left| \frac{1 - 4x^2}{2x + 1} - 2 \right| < \varepsilon
\]

\[
\left| \frac{1 - 4x^2}{2x + 1} - 2 \right| < \varepsilon
\]

即 \(\lim_{x \to -\frac{1}{2}} \frac{1 - 4x^2}{2x + 1} = 2\)。
