$$
f(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} f(x) = \lim_{x \to 0} \left(\frac{f(x)}{x} \cdot x\right) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} x = 0 \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} = 0,
$$

所以 (A) 正确；

由选项 (A) 知，$f(0) = 0$，所以 $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x}$ 存在，根据导数定义，

$$
f'(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x) - f(0)}{x - 0} \text{ 存在，所以 (C) 也正确；}
$$

由 $f(x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续，所以 $f(-x)$ 在 $x = 0$ 处连续，从而

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} [f(x) + f(-x)] = \lim_{x \to 0} f(x) + \lim_{x \to 0} f(-x) = f(0) + f(0) = 2f(0)
$$

所以 $2f(0) = \lim_{x \to 0} \left[\frac{f(x) + f(-x)}{x} \cdot x\right] = \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(-x)}{x} = 0 \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(x)}{x} + 0 \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{f(-x)}{x} = 0$
