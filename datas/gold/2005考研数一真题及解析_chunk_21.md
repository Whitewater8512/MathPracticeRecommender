\( D_2 = \{(x, y) | 1 \leq x^2 + y^2 \leq \sqrt{2}, x \geq 0, y \geq 0 \} \).

于是有 \(\left[1 + x^2 + y^2\right] = \begin{cases} 
1, & \text{当 } (x, y) \in D_1 \\
2, & \text{当 } (x, y) \in D_2 
\end{cases}\)

从而 \(\iint_{D} xy[1 + x^2 + y^2] \, dx \, dy = \iint_{D_1} xy \, dx \, dy + 2 \iint_{D_2} xy \, dx \, dy\) (二重积分对区域的可加性)

= \(\iint_{D_1} xy \, dx \, dy + 2 \iint_{D_2} xy \, dx \, dy\) (用极坐标把不同区域上的二重积分化为累次积分)

= \(\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \cos \theta \, d\theta \int_0^1 r^3 \, dr + 2 \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{2}} \sin \theta \cos \theta \, d\theta \int_1^{\sqrt{2}} r^3 \, dr\) (根据单一—莱公式)
