## 解

当 \(0 \leq t \leq 1\) 时, \(S(t) = \frac{1}{2}t^2\),

当 \(1 < t \leq 2\) 时, \(S(t) = 1 - \frac{1}{2}(2-t)^2 = -\frac{1}{2}t^2 + 2t - 1\),

当 \(t > 2\) 时, \(S(t) = 1\).

故

\[ S(t) = 
\begin{cases} 
\frac{1}{2}t^2, & 0 \leq t \leq 1, \\
-\frac{1}{2}t^2 + 2t - 1, & 1 < t \leq 2, \\
1, & t > 2.
\end{cases} \]
